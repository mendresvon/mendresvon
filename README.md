## Building software with a reason

When I left the Philippines to study in Taiwan, my little sister was three years old. She is turning eight this year. We do not get to be home together often, but whenever I return, watching movies is one of our favorite things to do.

I built [FilmFolio](https://github.com/mendresvon/FilmFolio) for her: a full-stack movie manager for discovering, saving, and keeping track of the films we watch together.

## About

I am a Filipino computer science student who enjoys building products that move from an idea to a deployed, usable system. My work spans full-stack development, cloud-native infrastructure, DevOps, and applied machine learning.

I am also involved in AI research, including mechanistic interpretability and multimodal emotion-recognition systems. I care about understanding not only whether a system works, but also how it works and who it is useful to.
